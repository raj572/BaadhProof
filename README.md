# BaadhProof 🌊

### Flood Impact Documentation & Relief Navigation Platform

BaadhProof is a mobile-friendly, Hindi-first platform concept designed to help flood-affected farmers and families in Bihar organize damage records, manage supporting evidence, understand document requirements, and find relevant official relief resources.

Our vision is to make the process of organizing flood-related information simpler and more accessible, with the potential to scale across flood-prone regions of India.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

After floods, affected families and farmers may struggle to organize damage information, preserve photographs and other evidence, understand required documents, and find the correct official assistance channels.

BaadhProof aims to bring these tasks together in one accessible digital platform.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

BaadhProof aims to provide a simple, guided workflow for documenting flood impacts and navigating relevant official resources.

### Key Features

- **Flood Damage Records:** Record incident dates, locations, affected crops, homes, livestock, and other losses.
- **Evidence Organization:** Attach photographs and notes to personal damage records.
- **Document Checklist:** View general checklists of information and documents that may be useful.
- **Official Resource Directory:** Find relevant government information and verified official links.
- **PDF Summary:** Generate a printable summary of user-entered information.
- **Mobile-Friendly Interface:** Designed for accessibility and use on smartphones.

## 👥 Target Users

- Flood-affected farmers in Bihar
- Families affected by flooding
- Community volunteers supporting affected residents
- Other flood-prone communities across India

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

Planned technologies:

- Frontend: React and TypeScript
- Styling: Tailwind CSS
- Backend and database: Supabase
- Deployment: Vercel
- Additional tools: PDF generation and private file storage

*The final stack may change during development.*

## 🚀 Development Roadmap

1. Design a Hindi-first, mobile-responsive interface.
2. Build the flood-impact record form and validation.
3. Add record management and optional photo uploads.
4. Create document checklists and an official resource directory.
5. Implement printable or downloadable PDF summaries.
6. Test usability, privacy, and accessibility.
7. Publish setup instructions and documentation.

## 📊 Expected Impact

BaadhProof aims to:

- Make flood damage information easier to organize.
- Help users prepare information before contacting relevant authorities.
- Improve access to official relief resources.
- Support a scalable, technology-enabled approach to disaster resilience.

Impact will be measured through prototype testing and feedback from intended users.

## 🔐 Privacy and Disclaimer

BaadhProof is an independent project concept and is not an official government portal.

User-submitted records are not official damage certificates, verified assessments, or guarantees of compensation. Users should confirm eligibility, document requirements, and application procedures with the relevant authorities.

The application should collect only necessary information and protect personal records and uploaded evidence.
